# Peer-Reviewed Foundations for the Aero-FF Hypothesis

## The verified phenomenon

**Liu, S., Ge, H., et al. "Generation of spatiotemporal acoustic vortices with
arbitrarily oriented orbital angular momentum." Nature Communications 16:2823 (2025).**
DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-58154-1

This peer-reviewed, experimentally verified paper demonstrates:
- 3D spatiotemporal acoustic vortices carrying longitudinal and transverse OAM
- Arbitrarily oriented OAM through rotation and intersection of vortices
- Experimental generation using a 121-speaker phased array
- OAM direction as a new degree of freedom in acoustic wave manipulation

## The connection to Aero-FF

The Aero-FF hypothesis claims that acoustic OAM-carrying helicoidal Rayleigh
surface waves can regularize 3D fluid vorticity. The Liu et al. paper provides
the peer-reviewed experimental foundation: acoustic vortices with controlled
OAM ARE physically real and experimentally demonstrable.

What remains HYPOTHESIS (not established by the peer-reviewed literature):
- That the OAM regularization prevents Navier-Stokes velocity blowup
- That the Aero-FF device generates the claimed topological protection
- That the 4-step kinetic chain produces the claimed stable Coandă boundary
